About

I am a PhD candidate at the National Technical University of Athens and the Archimedes Research Unit at Athena Research Center, advised by Dimitris Fotakis. I completed my undergraduate studies at the School of Applied Mathematical and Physical Sciences of NTUA. My research focuses on online algorithms under beyond worst-case assumptions, including prophet inequalities and random-order models.

Research interests

  • Online Algorithms
  • Mechanism Design
  • Beyond Worst-Case Analysis
